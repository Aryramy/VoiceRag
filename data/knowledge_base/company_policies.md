# Apex Global Knowledge Base: Policies, Products, and Support

## 1. Return and Refund Policy
Apex Global offers a strict 30-day return policy from the date of product delivery. 
To be eligible for a return, the product must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after our inspection warehouse receives and approves the returned item.
Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part or a defective product out of the box.

## 2. Warranty and Hardware Replacement
All hardware items purchased directly through Apex Global come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental drops, liquid immersion, unauthorized modifications, or third-party repair attempts.
For warranty claims, customers should contact technical support with serial numbers and photographic proof of the issue.

## 3. Shipping Options and Delivery Timelines
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States.
Expedited next-day shipping is available for orders placed before 1:00 PM Eastern Time Monday through Friday.
International shipping is currently supported to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union, with customs clearance typically taking 7 to 10 business days.

## 4. Voice AI System Support Hours
The Apex automated voice support system operates 24/7/365 to handle common queries, order tracking, and RMA requests.
Human Tier-2 support engineers are available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time.
Customers requesting escalation during off-hours will have a callback ticket generated automatically for the next business morning.
